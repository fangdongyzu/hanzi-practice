WELCOME TO YOUR WRITING STUDIO

A small ritual.
A lasting skill.

  1. Bring your own words. Paste up to 200 Chinese characters. Punctuation and other text are ignored; repeated characters stay in your lesson.
  2. Watch. Play the stroke animation or select a numbered step to see a single stroke.
  3. Trace. Follow the pale outline with a finger, stylus, or mouse. Hints appear after two mistakes.
  4. Write. Start on a blank grid. No outlines, automatic hints, or stroke previews. Only the correct next stroke, drawn in the right direction, is accepted.

Progress is saved locally for Trace and Write separately. Select a character to revisit it. Printable sheets provide paper practice; stroke order can only be checked on screen.

Character shapes and stroke orders follow the Make Me a Hanzi dataset and can differ from regional teaching standards. Some rare characters are unavailable. New character data requires an internet connection.
